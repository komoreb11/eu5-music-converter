Brings the music of Europa Universalis IV into EU5 — fully integrated into the game's dynamic music engine. War, peace, and cultural context all trigger the right tracks automatically, just like in the original games.

[hr]

[h2]What it does[/h2]

[b]179 tracks[/b] from EU4 and its music DLCs are woven into EU5's adaptive music system:

[list]
[*][b]At war[/b] — EU4 war tracks play alongside EU5 music
[*][b]At peace[/b] — EU4 ambient and atmospheric tracks
[*][b]Cultural context[/b] — regional EU4 music matches your nation's culture group:
[list]
[*]European nations hear British, French, HRE, Scandinavian, Russian, Iberian, Caucasian tracks
[*]Ottoman/Persian/Egyptian/Arab/Central Asian → Middle Eastern tracks
[*]Chinese/Japanese → East Asian tracks
[*]Indian and South-East Asian → Indian tracks
[*]African, North and South American nations get their own regional tracks
[/list]
[/list]

[quote]EU4 track names won't appear in the Music Player list. The music plays automatically in the background based on game state — you'll just hear it change. You can still skip to the next track using the [b]Next[/b] button in the Music Player. This is intentional to keep the mod multiplayer-compatible (no checksum change).[/quote]

[hr]

[h2]Requirements — read before installing[/h2]

[b]1. Europa Universalis IV[/b] must be installed on the same Steam account.
The mod reads audio files directly from your EU4 folder — it does not download or redistribute any music.
Only tracks from DLCs you own will be included.

[b]2. FFmpeg[/b]
Audio conversion tool.
[b]Windows:[/b] install via terminal:
[code]winget install ffmpeg[/code]
Or download from [url=https://ffmpeg.org]ffmpeg.org[/url] and add to PATH.
[b]Linux / Steam Deck:[/b] install FFmpeg with your package manager (e.g. [code]sudo apt install ffmpeg[/code]). Python 3 is also required (already included in SteamOS).

[hr]

[h2]Installation[/h2]

[b]Step 1.[/b] Make sure requirements 1 and 2 above are installed.

[b]Step 2.[/b] In Steam → right-click EU5 → Properties → [b]Launch Options[/b], paste:

[b]Windows:[/b]
[code]cmd /c "curl -sfL -o %TEMP%\eu4launch.cmd https://raw.githubusercontent.com/komoreb11/eu5-music-converter/main/launch.cmd & call %TEMP%\eu4launch.cmd %command%"[/code]

[b]Linux / Steam Deck (Proton):[/b]
[code]bash -c 'f="$HOME/.cache/eu4snd_setup.sh"; mkdir -p "$HOME/.cache"; curl -sfL -o "$f.tmp" https://raw.githubusercontent.com/komoreb11/eu5-music-converter/main/EU4_Soundtrack_Setup.sh && mv "$f.tmp" "$f"; bash "$f" "$@"' _ %command%[/code]

[b]Step 3.[/b] Launch EU5. A setup window will appear and convert all EU4 tracks (~2–5 minutes). The game starts automatically when done.

[b]Step 4.[/b] Once in the game, open the mods menu → enable [b]EU4 Soundtrack for EU5[/b] → restart the game.

After the first conversion FFmpeg is no longer required. [b]Keep the launch option[/b]: it delivers mod updates and keeps the sound banks in sync with your tracks, EU5 patches and new DLCs.

[hr]

[h2]How the music works[/h2]

The setup builds the mod's Wwise sound banks on your PC from your EU5 installation, adding EU4 tracks to EU5's WAR, PEACE and cultural playlists. No EU5 tracks are replaced. Only tracks you have are added — no silent gaps for DLCs you don't own.

[list]
[*][b]WAR[/b] — +146 EU4 tracks (player at war)
[*][b]PEACE[/b] — +135 EU4 tracks (player at peace)
[*][b]European culture[/b] (incl. Caucasus) — +48 tracks
[*][b]Middle Eastern culture[/b] (Ottoman/Persian/Arab/Central Asian) — +19 tracks
[*][b]East Asian culture[/b] (Chinese/Japanese/etc.) — +13 tracks
[*][b]Indian culture[/b] (incl. South-East Asia) — +6 tracks
[*][b]African culture[/b] — +4 tracks
[*][b]North American culture[/b] — +1 track
[*][b]South American culture[/b] (incl. Aztec/Maya) — +4 tracks
[/list]

Neutral and discovery tracks appear in both WAR and PEACE playlists, so they play regardless of war state.

Regional tracks are also added to their cultural playlist, where they alternate with EU5 soloist pieces.

[b]Music Player modes:[/b] Dynamic – All mixes both, Dynamic – Cinematic is war/peace only, Dynamic/Static – Cultural are mostly cultural. Static – Cinematic has no EU4 tracks.

[h2]Troubleshooting[/h2]

[b]"Game crashes when clicking Accept with mod checked in the mod menu"[/b] — Try changing the mod's load order. Place EU4 Soundtrack for EU5 earlier in the list — this usually fixes the crash or try another order. After changes restart the game as well.

[b]"FFmpeg not found"[/b] — Install FFmpeg (see Requirements), then restart Steam.

[b]"EU4 not found" / "EU5 not found"[/b] — The script finds the games automatically through your Steam libraries. Make sure both are installed on the same Steam account.

[b]"packed_codebooks.bin download failed"[/b] — Check your internet connection.

[b]Brief sound then silence / silent gaps[/b] — Make sure the launch option is set and start the game once.

[b]Music not playing[/b] — Make sure the mod is enabled and the game was restarted after enabling it

[b]Linux: nothing seems to happen[/b] — Steam shows no console; see [code]~/.cache/eu4snd/setup.log[/code].
